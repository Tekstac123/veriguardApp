# Compliance Circular: Interim Controls on UPI Mule Accounts
*Deccan Commonwealth Bank - synthetic training data*

## 1. Background

Following an increase in mule account activity, the following interim controls apply until 31 December 2026.

## 2. Interim Rule

An alert is generated when an account receives 25 or more inbound UPI or IMPS credits from distinct senders within 7 days and transfers out 80 percent or more of the credited value within 24 hours. Accounts of customers aged under 25 or with occupation 'Student' are prioritised as High.
