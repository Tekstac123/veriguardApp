# Escalation and Approval Matrix
*Deccan Commonwealth Bank - synthetic training data*

## 1. Approval Authorities

Close alert as false positive (Low or Medium band): Analyst. Close investigation with no further action (Medium band): Investigator. Close investigation (High band): Principal Officer. Escalate for STR consideration: Investigator recommends, Principal Officer decides. File STR: Principal Officer only. Restrict or freeze account: Principal Officer with Head of Operations. Continue relationship with PEP under investigation: Head of Compliance.

## 2. AI-Assisted Decisions

Where an AI system produces a recommendation, the approval authority is unchanged. The approver must see the recommendation, the risk factor breakdown and the cited evidence. Approval or rejection, and the approver's reason, are logged.
