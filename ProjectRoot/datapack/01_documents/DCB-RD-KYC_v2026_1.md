# Regulatory Digest: RBI KYC Directions (Training Summary)
*Deccan Commonwealth Bank - synthetic training data*

## 0. Disclaimer

This is a DCB-authored training summary, not regulatory text. Always verify against the official source before relying on it.

## 1. Risk-Based Approach

Regulated entities must categorise customers by risk and apply due diligence proportionate to that risk.

## 2. Periodic Updation

KYC must be updated at least once every 2 years for high risk, once every 8 years for medium risk and once every 10 years for low risk customers.

## 3. Beneficial Ownership

For companies, beneficial owners are natural persons holding more than 10 percent of shares, capital or profits, or exercising control through other means.

## 4. Video-based Customer Identification

Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is permitted as an alternative to in-person verification, subject to prescribed safeguards.
