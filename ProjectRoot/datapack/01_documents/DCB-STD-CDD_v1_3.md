# Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence Standard
*Deccan Commonwealth Bank - synthetic training data*

## 1. Standard CDD

Standard CDD requires verification of identity and address, capture of occupation or business activity, expected monthly transaction volume, and purpose of the relationship. For legal entities, beneficial owners holding more than 10 percent of shares or profits must be identified and verified.

## 2. Triggers for Enhanced Due Diligence

EDD is mandatory when: (a) the customer is High Risk; (b) the customer is a PEP or related to a PEP; (c) the customer has exposure to a jurisdiction on the DCB High-Risk Jurisdiction Register; (d) actual monthly turnover exceeds the declared expected volume by more than 200 percent for two consecutive months; or (e) an investigation is opened on the customer.

## 3. EDD Measures

EDD measures include source of wealth and source of funds verification, adverse media screening, site visit for business customers, senior approval for continuation, and increased monitoring frequency. EDD findings are recorded in the customer file within 10 working days of the trigger.

## 4. Declared versus Actual Activity

Investigators compare actual account activity against the expected monthly volume declared at onboarding. A sustained deviation is not by itself evidence of suspicion. Legitimate explanations such as salary revisions, annual bonuses, sale of assets or employee stock option proceeds must be considered and documented.
