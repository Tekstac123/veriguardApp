# Regulatory Digest: PMLA Reporting Obligations (Training Summary)
*Deccan Commonwealth Bank - synthetic training data*

## 0. Disclaimer

This is a DCB-authored training summary, not regulatory text. Always verify against the official source before relying on it.

## 1. Cash Transaction Report (CTR)

Cash transactions above Rs 10 lakh, or a series of integrally connected cash transactions that together exceed Rs 10 lakh within a month, are reported to FIU-IND by the 15th of the following month.

## 2. Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)

An STR is filed with FIU-IND not later than 7 working days after the Principal Officer is satisfied that a transaction is suspicious. Attempted transactions are also reportable.

## 3. Cross-Border Wire Transfer Report (CBWTR)

Cross-border wire transfers above Rs 5 lakh, where either the origin or destination is in India, are reported by the 15th of the following month.

## 4. Record Keeping

Transaction and identity records are maintained for at least 5 years.

## 5. Confidentiality

Reporting entities and their staff must keep the filing of STRs confidential and must not tip off the customer.
